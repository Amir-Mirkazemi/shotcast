Every stored match file is checked before it is used: two distinct teams with exactly one home side, scores that are whole numbers, an ISO date, player rows only for those two teams, no player with more shots on target than shots (a goal with no listed shot is allowed), minutes between 0 and 150, and only known shot outcomes. A file that fails is quarantined with its reasons and left out of every page.
